# Candidates for a later pass

Bellringers, grave mimics and directional audio are now implemented. The other entries remain candidates for a later pass.

## Mobs

- **Bellringer (implemented):** watches a courtyard or junction. On spotting you, it visibly winds up a bell ring. Break sight before the ring or nearby enemies investigate your location. Adds pressure using the existing population rather than spawning a crowd. This is the strongest next candidate.
- **Pallbearers:** a slow procession carries a coffin along a predictable route. They ignore you unless you touch the procession or sprint too close. Let them pass, follow behind, or use another corridor. An environmental threat rather than another pursuer.
- **Grave mimic (implemented):** disturbed earth occasionally has a tell: soil rises and falls like breathing, and the compass needle trembles nearby. The first dig wakes it with a clear escape window. Back away before it opens. The tell must be reliable; no unavoidable damage or random punishment for digging.
- **Lantern moths:** visibly gather around a bright lantern and consume fuel while attached. Briefly shutter the lamp to disperse them, exposing you to other enemies. Uses the existing F control. Only worth adding if fuel balance can support it.

## Other suggestions

- **Directional sound (implemented):** footsteps for zombies, chains for ghosts, armor before a Gravebound charge. Let sound communicate the enemy and direction before it reaches the beam. This is the highest-priority polish suggestion.
- **Run-local shortcuts:** a few gates can be unbarred from one side. Exploring a distant room can create a faster route home, making knowledge of the cemetery valuable.
- **Treasure sets:** a modest banking bonus for bringing back three different artifact types. This makes the new loot variety influence the decision to stay or leave without permanent power upgrades.
- **Collection record:** show recovered artifact varieties and best haul after extraction. Keep it cosmetic so returning players do not gain an unfair advantage.

## Accepted, deferred mystery change

The black lantern goes in a secret room concealed behind another room. Dig a key at a fixed map location to unlock that room. The key spawns only after the appropriate mystery milestone, independently of random treasure. This replaces the old starting-lamp/hatch access chain when the mystery receives its next pass. No live story implementation is changed in this gameplay round.
